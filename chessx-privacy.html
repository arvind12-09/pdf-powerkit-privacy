ChessX provides offline chess gameplay, including games against the built-in computer opponent and local two-player games. The App does not require users to create an account to play.

1. Information We Handle

1.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

ChessX stores game information and preferences in the App's local Android storage so that it can save and restore games and remember your settings. This information may include:

  • Current chess match and saved game progress.
  • Chess moves and move history.
  • White and black player clock information.
  • Selected game time and match settings.
  • Game mode and computer difficulty preferences.
  • Computer player color preference.
  • Captured chess pieces and related match information.
  • Sound preferences and selected chessboard theme.

We do not operate a ChessX account system or our own cloud server for storing chess matches. ChessX does not send your locally saved chess moves or match history to a ChessX-operated server.

Local App data may be removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling ChessX. However, Android backup or device-transfer features may back up or restore some App data depending on your device, Android version and backup settings. A backup may remain available even after the App is uninstalled.

1.2 Information Processed by Advertising Services

ChessX uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner, interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements.

When the advertising SDK is used, Google may automatically collect and process certain information for purposes such as delivering and measuring advertisements, preventing fraud and abuse, and maintaining the advertising service. Depending on the SDK version, configuration, your device settings and applicable consent choices, this information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to infer a general or approximate location.
  • Advertising ID and other device or account identifiers.
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system and related technical information.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as ad requests, ad views, taps and rewarded-ad interactions.
  • Diagnostic, performance and crash-related information.

The information processed and the purposes for which it is used may vary according to Google's services, your location, device settings and advertising privacy choices. ChessX does not request Android device-location permission for its chess gameplay.

Google processes information under its own terms and privacy policies. For information about how Google uses data from apps that use its services, visit Google's information about partner sites and apps.

1.3 Consent Management and Privacy Choices

ChessX integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage advertising consent and privacy choices where applicable. The App requests updated consent information and, when required by the consent framework, may display a consent message.

ChessX checks whether ads may be requested before initializing or requesting advertisements. Where a privacy-options entry point is required and made available by the consent framework, you can use the Privacy Options feature in the App to review or change the choices presented there.

The messages and options available may depend on your location, applicable requirements and the consent messages configured for the advertising service.

2. How Information Is Used

Information handled by ChessX and its service providers may be used to:

  • Save and restore chess matches on your device.
  • Remember game settings and preferences.
  • Display and measure banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements.
  • Manage advertising consent and privacy choices.
  • Maintain app functionality, performance and stability.
  • Check for available app updates through Google Play.
  • Support advertising security and fraud prevention.

We do not sell your locally stored chess match information or operate our own advertising network.

4. Internet Connection and Offline Gameplay

ChessX gameplay works without an internet connection. You can play against the built-in computer opponent or another person on the same device while offline. Chess move calculations and locally saved game data do not require an online ChessX server.

An internet connection may be used to:

  • Load and display advertisements.
  • Refresh advertising consent information and load consent messages where required.
  • Check for and obtain app updates through Google Play.

Advertisements, consent updates and online update checks may not work when an internet connection is unavailable. Offline chess gameplay remains available.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. ChessX does not operate its own server for collecting or sharing your locally stored chess match history.

Information processed by Google Mobile Ads, UMP or Google Play may be received or processed by Google and relevant service providers as necessary to provide advertising, consent management, app distribution and update services. Such processing is subject to the applicable third-party policies and settings.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

ChessX game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. You can remove this local App data by clearing ChessX's storage through Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

Android backup and device-transfer settings may affect whether some App data is backed up or later restored. Removing the App does not necessarily delete a backup that was previously created.

Information processed by Google AdMob, UMP or Google Play is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion practices. We do not have direct control over information retained by Google. You can review Google's privacy controls and available options at Google Account – Data and Privacy.

7. Children's Privacy

ChessX is not designed or marketed for children under the age specified in the App's Google Play target-audience settings. The App does not provide a user registration system or ask players to submit personal profile information to us to play chess.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us using the email address in Section 11.

8. Data Security

ChessX stores game information in Android's application storage and does not operate its own cloud database for chess matches. We use third-party services for advertising, consent management and app updates. No method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

The security and handling of information processed by Google services are described in Google's applicable privacy and security documentation.

  • Reviewing or changing advertising consent choices through the App's Privacy Options feature when available.
  • Managing advertising and privacy settings provided by your Android device or Google account.
  • Clearing locally stored ChessX information through Android settings.
  • Contacting us with questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy.

For information processed by Google, you may need to exercise applicable privacy rights directly with Google through its privacy controls and request channels.
