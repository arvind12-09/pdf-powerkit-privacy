This policy explains how the ChessX app (package com.rookora.chess) handles information.

1. Information we do not collect

ChessX has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, photos, camera or microphone data, contacts, or files. The developer does not run any server that receives your data.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue a game, the app saves your current match on your device: moves, clock times, captured pieces, game mode, computer difficulty level, and your sound setting. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

ChessX shows ads through Google AdMob, a third-party service. To show and measure ads, AdMob and Google may collect and use information from your device, such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Device and operating system information
  • App and ad interaction data (for example, ad views and clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy, not by us. Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

The app shows banner ads, interstitial ads (full screen, between games) and rewarded ads (optional video ads you choose to watch for an extra undo).

4. Your choices

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or other regions that require it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset it or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Ads in general: you can stop all data collection by uninstalling the app.

5. Internet use

The app needs internet only to load ads. All chess play, including the computer opponent, works offline.

6. Children's privacy

ChessX is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data security and retention

We do not hold your data. Game data is kept only on your device. Data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google under its own policies.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
