ChessX is an offline chess game intended for users aged 18 and above. The application is currently distributed through Google Play in India.

1. Information We Collect

ChessX does not require users to create an account, register, or provide their name, phone number, or personal profile information to play chess.

The core chess gameplay works offline. However, the application uses third-party advertising services, which may collect and process certain information as described in this Privacy Policy.

1.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

ChessX stores certain game information and preferences locally on your device to provide its features. This may include:

  • Current chess match and saved game progress.
  • Chess moves and move history.
  • White and black player clock information.
  • Selected game time and match settings.
  • Game mode and difficulty preferences.
  • Computer player color preference.
  • Captured chess pieces and related match information.
  • Sound preferences.
  • Selected chessboard theme.

This information is stored using Android's local application storage and is used to restore your match and remember your preferences.

We do not operate a ChessX account system or our own cloud server for storing chess matches.

Locally stored information may remain on your device until you clear the application's data or uninstall the application. Android device backup features may also handle application data according to your device and account settings.

1.2 Advertising Information

ChessX uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including:

  • Banner advertisements.
  • Interstitial advertisements.
  • Rewarded advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information for advertising, analytics, security, and fraud prevention purposes.

Depending on the SDK configuration and applicable settings, this information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Advertising ID and other device or account identifiers.
  • App launches, taps, video views, and other app interactions.
  • Advertisement interaction information.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.
  • Information about app or SDK performance and stability.

The information collected and how it is used may depend on Google's services, your device settings, your location, and your advertising privacy choices.

Google processes information according to its own privacy policies and applicable settings.

1.3 Consent Management and Privacy Choices

ChessX integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage advertising consent and privacy choices where required.

When required, the UMP SDK may display a consent message before advertisements are requested. ChessX checks the available consent status before initializing advertising services and requesting ads.

Depending on your location and applicable privacy requirements, you may be able to review or change your advertising privacy choices through the Privacy Options feature in the App's Settings menu.

The availability of consent forms and privacy options may vary according to Google's requirements and your location.

2. How We Use Information

Information handled by ChessX and its third-party service providers may be used for the following purposes:

  • Saving and restoring chess matches.
  • Remembering game settings and preferences.
  • Displaying banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.
  • Managing advertising consent and privacy choices.
  • Maintaining app functionality and stability.
  • Supporting app updates through Google Play.
  • Supporting advertising security and fraud prevention.

We do not sell locally stored chess match information.
