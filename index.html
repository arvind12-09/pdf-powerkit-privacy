Privacy Policy

PDF PowerKit

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF PowerKit handles information when you use its PDF, scanning, OCR, document-conversion, editing and related features.

Effective date September 20, 2026
Privacy contact arvind.mns18@gmail.com

1. Files you choose

PDF PowerKit allows you to select PDF files, images and supported Word documents for document-processing features such as creating, editing, converting, merging, splitting, compressing, protecting, unlocking, redacting, signing, watermarking, scanning and related PDF operations.

PDF PowerKit does not operate its own server or user database for your documents and does not require you to upload your documents to a PDF PowerKit server.

Files selected by you are processed according to the feature you choose. Many operations are designed to run on your device. Some features use Google-provided or other third-party components required to provide the requested functionality.

Generated files may be saved to the Android Downloads folder or another location used by the applicable feature.

2. Personal information

PDF PowerKit does not require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number or email address in order to use the PDF tools.

The app does not provide a user account, username/password system or developer-operated personal user database.

If you contact PDF PowerKit support, information you voluntarily include in your message may be received by the developer so that your request can be answered.

3. File access and permissions

PDF PowerKit uses Android's file and document-selection mechanisms to let you choose files for supported operations. Selecting a file does not by itself give the app unrestricted access to every file on your device.

The app requests camera access only for features that require the device camera.

4. Camera and scanning

Camera-based scanning features use the device camera and require camera permission.

Camera images may be temporarily stored in the application's cache while scanning or processing is taking place. Temporary files may be removed after processing where applicable. Exact temporary-file behavior can vary by feature.

5. OCR and machine-learning features

PDF PowerKit includes OCR using Google ML Kit text-recognition components, including the supported language models used by the app.

PDF PowerKit does not operate its own server for uploading OCR documents or extracted text. The app processes OCR input through the ML Kit APIs included or used by the application.

Google-provided machine-learning components are subject to applicable Google terms, privacy documentation and technical behavior of those services.

6. Document Scanner

Some scanning functionality uses Google's ML Kit Document Scanner through Google Play services. This component is provided by Google and may have its own processing, privacy practices and technical requirements.

PDF PowerKit does not operate its own server for uploading scanned documents.

7. Advertising

PDF PowerKit uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner advertisements.

According to Google's current Android disclosure for Google Mobile Ads SDK version 25.5.0, the SDK may automatically collect and share certain information for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general device location;
  • user product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information related to app and SDK performance, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage; and
  • device and account identifiers, including Android advertising ID and app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers associated with accounts on the device.

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

Android advertising ID collection is optional and can be affected by Android user settings and applicable advertising configurations.

PDF PowerKit does not intentionally provide the contents of a PDF, image or Word document to Google Mobile Ads merely for displaying an advertisement.

8. Advertising privacy and consent

PDF PowerKit uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), where applicable, to request and manage advertising consent and privacy choices.

Depending on your location, applicable law and the consent configuration for the app, a privacy or consent message may be displayed. Available choices can vary by location and applicable privacy requirements.

PDF PowerKit also provides an Advertising Privacy option in Settings where applicable Google privacy choices can be reviewed or managed.

9. Local application settings and history

PDF PowerKit stores certain application preferences locally on your device. These may include theme preference, favorite tools, favorite ordering, tool-layout preference and terms-acceptance status.

The app also maintains local file-history information for supported generated files. This history is stored locally and may include the file name, feature name, generated-file URI and creation time so the app can display its history interface.

10. Local file processing and generated files

Many PDF operations are designed to process selected documents and images on the device using Android and bundled libraries.

Generated files may be written to Android's Downloads storage using Android's MediaStore APIs. Files that you save may remain on your device until you move or delete them.

PDF PowerKit does not operate its own backend server for document storage, document processing or user accounts.

11. Data sharing

PDF PowerKit does not require an account and does not operate a developer-controlled database for storing user documents or personal profiles.

The app does not intentionally sell users' documents or personal information.

However, third-party services used by the application, including Google advertising, consent-management and scanning components, may collect, process or share information as necessary to provide their services and according to their applicable policies and the privacy choices available to you.

12. Security

PDF PowerKit uses Android's normal application, permission and file-access security mechanisms. Information transmitted by third-party services is protected according to the security mechanisms used by those services.

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

No software, storage system or method of electronic transmission can guarantee absolute security. You should keep backups of important documents.

13. Children's privacy

PDF PowerKit is not specifically designed for children. The app does not knowingly require children to create an account or provide personal information in order to use its PDF tools.

14. Data retention and deletion

PDF PowerKit does not maintain a user account or a developer-operated server containing users' documents or personal profiles.

Files created or saved by the app may remain on the user's device or in a location selected by the user until the user moves or deletes them. Temporary application files may be removed by the app where applicable, and Android may manage application cache according to its normal system behavior.

Third-party services used by the app may retain information according to their own policies and applicable legal requirements.

15. Third-party services and policies

PDF PowerKit uses third-party software and services, including Google Mobile Ads, Google User Messaging Platform, Google ML Kit text-recognition components and Google ML Kit Document Scanner.

Those services may have their own terms, privacy policies and data-handling practices. You should review the applicable Google documentation for details about their processing.

16. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality, third-party services or applicable legal requirements change. The effective date at the beginning of this policy indicates the date of the current version.

If a future version of PDF PowerKit adds accounts, cloud document processing, analytics, crash reporting, payments, additional advertising networks or other material data practices, this policy will be updated to describe those practices.
