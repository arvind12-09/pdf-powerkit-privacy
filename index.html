1. About PDF PowerKit

PDF PowerKit is an Android document utility application offering PDF creation, editing, conversion, scanning, compression, merging, splitting, page management, protection, unlocking, redaction, OCR, signatures, watermarks, and related document operations.

The application does not require an account. The developer does not operate a document-storage cloud, user-account database, or general-purpose backend server for processing your documents. The application does use third-party services, including Google services and YouTube, which may process information as described below.

2. Files you select, process, or create

You may select PDFs, images, Word documents, and other supported files using Android file-access interfaces. PDF PowerKit is designed to process documents locally on your device and the developer does not intentionally upload document contents to a PDF PowerKit server.

Depending on the feature, the application may read selected files, create temporary processing files, generate output files, save outputs to a location you select (including Downloads), and maintain local metadata about generated files. Files saved outside the application's private storage remain subject to Android and the selected storage location.

Please note: Local processing does not mean that no information ever leaves your device. Advertising, consent, scanning, update, embedded-video, and other third-party services may communicate with their providers.

3. Camera and document scanning

PDF PowerKit may request camera permission for camera-based scanning. CameraX may capture images, which may be temporarily stored in application cache during processing. Camera access is used for requested scanning functionality and is not intentionally used for advertising.

The app also provides document scanning through Google's ML Kit Document Scanner via Google Play services. The scanner can produce PDF or image results. The developer does not operate its own server for storing scanned documents. Google-provided components remain subject to Google's terms and privacy practices.

4. OCR and text recognition

PDF PowerKit includes Google ML Kit text-recognition libraries, including Latin and Devanagari recognition. When you use OCR, the selected or captured image is processed using on-device text-recognition functionality.

The image data submitted for recognition and the resulting recognized text are processed on your device and are not sent to Google's servers by ML Kit's on-device text-recognition APIs.

However, Google ML Kit may collect and transmit certain technical information to Google for diagnostics, usage analytics, performance monitoring, maintenance and improvement of its services. This information may include device manufacturer and model, Android version, app package name and version, per-installation identifiers, performance measurements, API configuration, input and output sizes, feature usage events, and error codes.

This technical information is distinct from the document images and recognized text. Google encrypts the transmission of collected ML Kit information in transit.

Temporary cached images may be created during processing and are deleted where the relevant processing operation completes successfully. The developer does not operate an OCR server or intentionally upload document contents to a PDF PowerKit server for OCR.

For more information, please refer to Google ML Kit Terms and Privacy and Google ML Kit Android Data Disclosure.

5. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

PDF PowerKit uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements. Depending on your device, Google's configuration, services used, consent choices, and applicable requirements, Google Mobile Ads may process IP address (which may estimate general location), device or advertising identifiers, ad impressions and clicks, diagnostics, crash and performance information, and information needed for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and security.

The exact information processed may vary. PDF PowerKit does not intentionally send PDF, image, or Word document contents to Google Mobile Ads for advertising purposes. Google processes information under its own policies and terms.

  • Google AdMob data and privacy information

6. Advertising consent and privacy choices

PDF PowerKit uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present and manage advertising-related consent choices where applicable. The options may vary by location, applicable requirements, Google's configuration, and available services.

Where available, the Advertising Privacy option in Settings may be used to review or change advertising privacy choices. These choices do not determine whether you can use core PDF-processing features. Google's processing remains governed by its policies and terms.

7. Local settings and generated-file history

The app stores certain information locally, which may include theme and application preferences, advertising consent-related status, scanner and feature preferences, and other settings required for operation.

Generated-file history

The current implementation retains a maximum of 200 history entries. An entry may contain the generated file name, feature used, Android file URI, and creation date and time. History stores metadata, not a second copy of the document. Deleting a history entry does not necessarily delete the actual output file; you may need to delete it separately through Android. Local settings and history are not maintained in a PDF PowerKit cloud account.

8. Private Vault

The Private Vault allows you to import files into the application's private storage. A selected file is copied into application storage; file contents are encrypted using AES-GCM. Vault metadata may include original and encrypted file names, folder, MIME type, size, and creation date. A Vault PIN provides an additional in-app access control. Previewing or exporting an item may create a decrypted temporary copy.

Vault information is stored locally, not in a PDF PowerKit document cloud.

Important security information: The current implementation stores Vault-related key material and the Vault PIN in app-private preferences. These are local application data, not credentials held by an online account. This should not be treated as equivalent to hardware-backed key storage or a professionally audited password-management system.

Temporary decrypted files may remain in application cache or in a destination application after previewing or sharing, depending on the operation and Android's behaviour. Shared files are subject to the receiving application's practices. Deleting Vault items removes corresponding entries and encrypted files where deletion succeeds. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may permanently remove Vault data. Keep a separate secure copy of important files; do not use the Vault as the only copy.

9. Android backups and device transfers

PDF PowerKit currently allows Android backup functionality. Depending on Android version, device and Google backup settings, and device-transfer configuration, some application data may be included in backups or device-to-device transfers. This may include preferences, history information, and files in application-private storage, including Vault-related data.

Android backup and device-transfer services are provided by Android, Google, or the device manufacturer, not the PDF PowerKit developer. Their handling is subject to the relevant provider's policies and settings. If you do not want application data included, review backup settings on your Android device. Disabling backup at device level may affect other apps. Take particular care when enabling backups and transfers because Vault data may be sensitive.

PDF PowerKit may display tutorials using an embedded YouTube player. When you open or play a video, YouTube and Google may receive information needed to load and play it, potentially including device, network, playback, and usage-related information. YouTube's own terms and privacy practices apply; the developer does not control its independent data collection or retention.

The app uses Google Play services for certain features, including document scanning, and Google Play in-app update functionality where supported. Google Play services or Google Play may process technical information needed to provide these functions, such as app version, device and service information, and update-related information. Google's applicable terms and privacy practices apply.

12. Sharing files with other applications

Some features allow you to share or open a document using another application. When you choose this option, the selected file may be passed through Android sharing or file-access mechanisms. The receiving application may copy, store, transmit, or otherwise process the file under its own policy and terms. PDF PowerKit does not control what another app does after you share a file. Only share documents with applications and recipients you trust.

13. Contact and support messages

The app's contact or report-problem action may open an email composer. If you contact the developer, the developer may receive your email address and the information or attachments you choose to include. Support messages are used to respond to questions, investigate problems, and improve the app. Do not include passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

14. Information sharing and disclosure

The developer does not operate a PDF PowerKit document cloud or user-account database and does not intentionally sell your documents. Information may nevertheless be processed by third parties when their services are used, including Google Mobile Ads and UMP (advertising and choices), Google ML Kit and Google Play services (scanning), Google Play (updates), YouTube and Google (embedded tutorials), an app you select when sharing a document, and your email provider when you contact the developer.

These providers act under their own terms, privacy policies, and legal obligations. Their practices are not controlled by the developer. The developer may also disclose information where required by applicable law or valid legal process.

15. Data retention and deletion

The developer does not maintain a user account containing your documents. Files saved to your chosen location remain until you delete or move them. Generated history is stored locally and currently retains up to 200 entries. Local settings and history may be removed by clearing app data or using relevant in-app controls where available. Deleting a history entry does not necessarily delete the generated document. Vault items remain until deleted through the Vault or until application data is removed.

Temporary files are deleted where cleanup is implemented and succeeds; Android may clear cache according to its normal behaviour. A receiving app may retain a shared temporary file. Third parties' retention is governed by their own policies and legal obligations. Data included in Android backups or device transfers may remain subject to the backup provider's retention and deletion processes.

Uninstalling PDF PowerKit or clearing application data may remove locally stored app information, but does not necessarily remove files saved separately in Downloads, files shared with another app, or information held by third parties.

16. Data security

PDF PowerKit uses Android application storage and file-access mechanisms and encryption for files stored in the Private Vault. Security also depends on your device, Android version, device lock, backup configuration, other installed apps, and services you choose to use. No application, device, storage system, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

  • Keep your device and operating system updated.
  • Use a secure device lock.
  • Avoid sharing sensitive documents with untrusted apps.
  • Take care when enabling device backups.
  • Keep separate secure copies of important documents.
  • Do not rely on the Private Vault as the only copy of important information.
