1. About PDF PowerKit

PDF PowerKit is an Android document utility application offering PDF creation, editing, conversion, scanning, compression, merging, splitting, page management, protection, unlocking, redaction, OCR, signatures, watermarks, Voice to PDF, and related document operations.

The application does not require an account. The developer does not operate a document-storage cloud, user-account database, or general-purpose backend server for processing your documents. The application does use third-party services, including Google services and YouTube, which may process information as described below.

2. Files you select, process, or create

You may select PDFs, images, Word documents, and other supported files using Android file-access interfaces. PDF PowerKit is designed to process documents locally on your device, and the developer does not intentionally upload document contents to a PDF PowerKit server.

Depending on the feature, the application may read selected files, create temporary processing files, generate output files, save outputs to a location you select (including Downloads), and maintain local metadata about generated files. Files saved outside the application's private storage remain subject to Android and the selected storage location.

Please note: Local document processing does not mean that no information ever leaves your device. Advertising, consent, speech recognition, scanning, update, embedded-video, and other third-party services may communicate with their providers as described in this policy.

3. Camera and document scanning

PDF PowerKit may request camera permission for camera-based scanning. CameraX may capture images, which may be temporarily stored in application cache during processing. Camera access is used for requested scanning functionality and is not intentionally used for advertising.

The app also provides document scanning through Google's ML Kit Document Scanner via Google Play services. The scanner can produce PDF or image results. The developer does not operate its own server for storing scanned documents. Google-provided components remain subject to Google's terms and privacy practices.

4. OCR and text recognition

PDF PowerKit includes Google ML Kit text-recognition libraries, including Latin and Devanagari recognition. When you use OCR, the selected or captured image is processed using on-device text-recognition functionality.

The image data submitted for recognition and the resulting recognized text are processed on your device and are not sent to Google's servers by ML Kit's on-device text-recognition APIs.

However, Google ML Kit may collect and transmit certain technical information to Google for diagnostics, usage analytics, performance monitoring, maintenance and improvement of its services. This information may include device manufacturer and model, Android version, app package name and version, per-installation identifiers, performance measurements, API configuration, input and output sizes, feature usage events, and error codes.

This technical information is distinct from the document images and recognized text. Google encrypts the transmission of collected ML Kit information in transit.

Temporary cached images may be created during processing and are deleted where the relevant processing operation completes successfully. The developer does not operate an OCR server or intentionally upload document contents to a PDF PowerKit server for OCR.

For more information, please refer to Google ML Kit Terms and Privacy and Google ML Kit Android Data Disclosure.

4A. Voice to PDF and speech recognition

PDF PowerKit includes a Voice to PDF feature that allows you to dictate text using speech recognition, edit or format the recognized text, and create a PDF document. You may also add images and arrange content as part of creating your PDF.

Microphone permission and audio processing

The app requests microphone permission only when you choose to use voice input. Audio is captured only while you are actively using the dictation or speech-recognition feature. The app does not continuously listen to microphone audio in the background for this feature.

For speech recognition, the app passes audio to the speech-recognition service available through Android on your device. This may be a service provided by Google or another provider selected or configured on your device.

Depending on your device, Android settings, speech-recognition provider, and network configuration, audio may be transmitted to and processed by that provider's servers. An internet connection may be required. The provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to its processing of audio and related information.

For Google's privacy practices, please see the Google Privacy Policy.

Audio recordings and recognized text

PDF PowerKit does not upload voice recordings to its own servers, use voice recordings for advertising, or save audio recordings as files for this feature. The audio is provided to the Android speech-recognition service to perform the requested recognition.

Only the recognized text that you choose to keep is included in the PDF you create. You can review and edit the recognized text before creating your document. The resulting PDF is saved to the location you choose.

Microphone permission choices

You can deny microphone permission or withdraw it later through Android Settings. If microphone permission is denied or withdrawn, voice input will not be available, but other PDF PowerKit features can continue to work.

7. Local settings and generated-file history

The app stores certain information locally, which may include theme and application preferences, advertising consent-related status, scanner and feature preferences, and other settings required for operation.

Generated-file history

The current implementation retains a maximum of 200 history entries. An entry may contain the generated file name, feature used, Android file URI, and creation date and time. History stores metadata, not a second copy of the document. Deleting a history entry does not necessarily delete the actual output file; you may need to delete it separately through Android. Local settings and history are not maintained in a PDF PowerKit cloud account.

8. Private Vault

The Private Vault allows you to import files into the application's private storage. Vault files are encrypted using AES-256-GCM. The app uses a randomly generated AES-256 master encryption key to encrypt and decrypt Vault files.

The master encryption key is protected by a separate AES-GCM wrapping key managed by Android Keystore. The wrapped master key is stored in the app's private SharedPreferences, while the wrapping key is managed by Android Keystore. This arrangement helps protect the master encryption key while preserving access to existing encrypted Vault files.

If Android Keystore is reset or the wrapping key becomes unavailable on your device, Vault files may become permanently unreadable.

Vault PIN and biometric authentication

Users can create a 4–6 digit PIN to control access to the Private Vault. The PIN is stored in the application's private SharedPreferences and is used by the app to verify access. The PIN is separate from the encryption key and is not used to derive the Vault's encryption key.

PDF PowerKit may also offer fingerprint or other supported biometric authentication to unlock the Private Vault. Biometric verification is handled by Android and the device's biometric system. PDF PowerKit does not access or store fingerprint images, face images, or biometric templates. The app receives the authentication result from Android. Users can use their Vault PIN when biometric authentication is unavailable.

Vault storage and temporary files

Vault files and related metadata are stored locally in the application's private storage, not in a PDF PowerKit document cloud. Metadata may include original and encrypted file names, folder, MIME type, size, and creation date.

When a file is imported, a temporary copy is created during processing and the app attempts to delete it when the import operation finishes, whether it succeeds or fails. When you export a Vault file, the app temporarily decrypts it for the export operation and attempts to delete that temporary export file after the operation completes.

When you preview or open a Vault item, a decrypted temporary copy is created in the app's cache and passed to a compatible application through Android's file-sharing mechanism. The app attempts to delete preview cache files older than 24 hours when the Vault is unlocked. Files that are less than 24 hours old may remain in the cache. Android may also clear cache files. The receiving application may retain a copy according to its own behaviour and policies.

Files exported from the Vault are no longer protected by the Vault's encryption. They are saved to the destination you select and are subject to the security and privacy practices of that destination and any application that accesses them.

Important: Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may permanently remove Vault files, key information, and access to encrypted content. Keep a separate secure copy of important documents. Do not use the Vault as the only copy of important information.

9. Android backups and device transfers

PDF PowerKit disables Android's standard application backup through its application configuration using android:allowBackup="false". This setting is intended to prevent application data from being included in standard Android backup operations.

Device-to-device transfer and migration behaviour may vary depending on Android version, device manufacturer, and the transfer method. PDF PowerKit does not promise that Vault files, encryption keys, or other app-private data will transfer to a new device.

If you uninstall the app, clear its application data, or change devices without a separate copy of your Vault files and the required key information, your Vault content may become permanently inaccessible or be lost. Export important Vault files before uninstalling the app, clearing its data, or changing devices. Do not rely on Android backup or device transfer as a way to preserve Vault contents.

15. Data retention and deletion

The developer does not maintain a user account containing your documents. Files saved to your chosen location remain until you delete or move them. Generated history is stored locally and currently retains up to 200 entries. Local settings and history may be removed by clearing app data or using relevant in-app controls where available. Deleting a history entry does not necessarily delete the generated document. Vault items remain until deleted through the Vault or until application data is removed.

Temporary import files are deleted when the import operation finishes, whether it succeeds or fails. Temporary decrypted export files are deleted after the export operation's cleanup runs. Decrypted preview files older than 24 hours are targeted for cleanup when the Vault is unlocked. Cleanup is best-effort, and files may remain if deletion fails or cleanup has not yet run.However, decrypted preview files created in the app cache may remain until they are cleaned up by the app or Android. A receiving app may retain a shared or opened file.

PDF PowerKit does not save audio recordings as files for its Voice to PDF feature. Audio is passed to the Android speech-recognition service for the requested recognition. Any retention or further processing by that service is governed by the provider's own policies and settings.

Third parties' retention is governed by their own policies and legal obligations. Uninstalling PDF PowerKit or clearing application data may remove locally stored app information, but does not necessarily remove files saved separately in Downloads, files shared with another app, or information held by third parties.

16. Data security

PDF PowerKit uses Android application-private storage and Android file-access mechanisms. Files stored in the Private Vault are encrypted using AES-256-GCM, and the master encryption key is protected by a separate wrapping key managed by Android Keystore. The Vault PIN is stored separately in the app's private SharedPreferences and is not used to derive the encryption key. Biometric authentication, when used, is handled by Android's biometric system.

Security also depends on your device, Android version, device lock, backup and transfer configuration, other installed apps, and services you choose to use. Files exported from the Vault or opened in another application may no longer be protected by Vault encryption. No application, device, storage system, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

  • Keep your device and operating system updated.
  • Use a secure device lock.
  • Avoid sharing sensitive documents with untrusted apps.
  • Export and keep separate secure copies of important Vault documents.
  • Be careful when opening decrypted Vault files in other applications.
  • Do not rely on the Private Vault as the only copy of important information.
