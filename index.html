Privacy Policy – PDF PowerKit

Effective date: September 20, 2026
App: PDF PowerKit
Developer/Publisher: Arvind Kumar
Privacy contact: arvind.mns18@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF PowerKit handles information when you use the Android application.

1. What the app does

PDF PowerKit is a document utility app that provides features including PDF creation, editing, conversion, scanning, compression, merging, splitting, page management, protection, unlocking, redaction, OCR, signatures, watermarks, and related document operations.

The app does not require a user account and does not operate a developer-controlled server or database for storing user documents.

2. Files you select or create

You may choose PDFs, images, Word documents, and other supported files for processing.

The application normally accesses files only when you select them through Android's file/document interfaces or when you create/save output files.

The application is designed to perform its document-processing operations locally on the Android device. It does not intentionally upload the contents of your selected PDFs, images, or Word documents to a PDF PowerKit server.

Generated documents can be saved to locations such as Android's Downloads collection. Once a file has been saved there, it is under your control through Android's file-management facilities.

3. Camera and scanning

PDF PowerKit requests the Android CAMERA permission for camera-based scanning features.

CameraX is used to capture images for scanning. The source code stores captured camera images temporarily in the application's cache while processing is performed.

Camera access is used for the requested scanning functionality. PDF PowerKit does not use the camera for advertising.

4. OCR and document scanning (Google ML Kit)

PDF PowerKit includes Google ML Kit text-recognition libraries, including Latin and Devanagari text recognition.

The OCR implementation reads a selected or captured image and passes it to the on-device ML Kit text-recognition API. The image is processed locally on the device and is not intentionally uploaded to a PDF PowerKit server. Temporary cached images are deleted after processing where the relevant operation completes.

The text-recognition libraries included by this project are bundled ML Kit libraries. Google's documentation describes ML Kit's on-device APIs as running on the device and supporting offline operation. The app does not operate its own OCR server.

PDF PowerKit also uses Google's ML Kit Document Scanner through Google Play services. The scanner is used to provide document-scanning functionality and can produce PDF/JPEG results for the application. The application does not operate its own server for storing scanned documents.

Processing performed by Google-provided components is subject to the technical behavior and applicable privacy terms of those services.

5. Advertising (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

PDF PowerKit displays banner advertisements using the Google Mobile Ads SDK for Android. The project currently references Google Mobile Ads SDK version 25.5.0.

According to Google's documentation, the Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as:

  • Device or other IDs (for example, Advertising ID and device identifiers)
  • IP address
  • Approximate location (for example, derived from IP address or coarse location, where enabled)
  • App activity related to advertising (for example, ad impressions, clicks, and in-app events)
  • App info and performance data (for example, crash logs and diagnostics)
  • Device and app information such as device model, operating system version, and app version

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and security. Google states that data collected by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

PDF PowerKit does not intentionally send the contents of your PDFs, images, or Word documents to the Mobile Ads SDK for the purpose of displaying advertisements. Google may process advertising-related information according to its own policies and the user's available privacy choices.

For more information, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads data disclosure for Google Play
  • AdMob: Add a privacy policy URL

6. Advertising consent (Google UMP SDK)

PDF PowerKit integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. The app requests advertising consent information and, where required, can show Google's consent form. The app also exposes an Advertising Privacy setting that can invoke Google's privacy-options form when Google indicates that such options are required.

The availability and content of consent choices can vary according to the user's location, applicable regulations, and Google's consent configuration. Your choices regarding advertising and related processing are handled through the mechanisms provided by Google.

For more information, see:

  • Disclose to EEA users (Google Mobile Ads SDK)
  • Disclose to EEA users (next-gen SDK)

7. Local settings and history

The app stores certain settings locally on the device, including settings such as:

  • Selected theme and language
  • Consent status for advertising
  • Scanner and other feature preferences
  • Other user choices required for app functionality

The generated-file history is limited to 20 entries and stores metadata such as the generated file name, feature name, file URI, and creation time. It does not store a second copy of the PDF in the history database.

These local settings and history are used to provide application functionality and are not maintained in a PDF PowerKit cloud account.

8. Temporary and output files

PDF PowerKit can create temporary files during document processing and camera scanning. For camera scanning, the project creates temporary JPEG files in the application's cache directory. The application also creates output files in Android's Downloads collection for many document operations.

Deleting an in-app history record does not necessarily delete every copy of a document that you may have saved elsewhere. Where the app provides a delete-generated-file action, that action attempts to delete the referenced generated file. You remain responsible for managing copies of files stored in locations under your control.

9. Contact and support messages

The app provides a contact/report-problem action that opens an email composer. If you voluntarily contact the developer, the developer may receive the information contained in your email, including your email address and whatever information you choose to provide.

Please do not include passwords, payment information, or unnecessary sensitive information in a support message.

10. No developer-controlled accounts or document cloud

PDF PowerKit does not operate its own account system, advertising server, document cloud, or backend database. PDF PowerKit does not intentionally sell your documents.

However, third-party services integrated into the application may independently process information necessary to provide their services. The principal services identified in the current project include:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK – for displaying advertisements
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK – for advertising consent and privacy choices
  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition – for OCR
  • Google ML Kit Document Scanner via Google Play services – for document scanning

Third-party services are governed by their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

11. Security

PDF PowerKit uses Android application and file-access security mechanisms. Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

No application, device, storage system, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should maintain backups of important documents and avoid processing files on a device you do not trust.

12. Data retention and deletion

PDF PowerKit does not maintain a developer-operated user account containing your documents. Files saved by the application can remain on your device or in a user-selected storage location until you delete or move them.

Temporary files are deleted by relevant processing code where applicable, and Android may remove application cache according to its normal behavior. Local preferences and history can be removed by clearing application data or by using the relevant controls provided by the application. Generated documents stored in Downloads are separate files and may need to be deleted separately.

Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

13. Children's privacy

PDF PowerKit is not specifically designed as a child-directed application. The app does not require children to create an account or provide personal information to use its core PDF tools. The developer does not knowingly request children to provide personal information through the application.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, contact the developer at the address below.

14. Changes to this policy

PDF PowerKit uses third-party software and services. Their policies can change independently of this application. Relevant information can be found in the official documentation and policies of Google and the open-source projects listed in the application's third-party notices.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's functionality, third-party services, data practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this policy identifies the current version.
