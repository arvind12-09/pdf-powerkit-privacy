Privacy Notice – PDF PowerKit

Effective date: 13 November 2026

PDF PowerKit is a document-processing application developed by Arvind Kumar. This notice explains what information may be processed when you use the app, why it is processed, and how you can exercise your privacy choices and rights.

1. Information and purposes

A. Documents and files you select

  • Information: PDF files, document contents, file names and file information that you select.
  • Purpose: To provide PDF reading, merging, splitting, compression, conversion, editing, protection and other document-processing features you request.
  • Processing: Document processing is designed to take place locally on your device. PDF PowerKit does not operate a developer-controlled document-storage service.

B. Images and camera information

  • Information: Images you select or capture, and information needed to access them.
  • Purpose: Document scanning, image-to-PDF conversion, ID scanning and other image-related features you request.

C. OCR and extracted text

  • Information: Images submitted for text recognition and text extracted from them.
  • Purpose: To recognise and make text available through OCR-related features.

D. Generated-file history

  • Information: Generated file names, feature used, file references and timestamps.
  • Purpose: To display and manage your generated-file history.

E. Private Vault

  • Information: Files you import into the Vault and related file metadata.
  • Purpose: To store and retrieve files through the app's Private Vault feature.

F. App preferences

  • Information: Settings such as your selected app preferences.
  • Purpose: To remember your preferences and provide the app experience you select.

G. Advertising and device information

  • Information: Depending on the advertising configuration, Google advertising services may process IP address, advertising or device identifiers, app information, ad interactions, diagnostics and related technical information.
  • Purpose: Advertising delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention.
  • Service: Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent-management services.

H. Support communications

  • Information: Your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send.
  • Purpose: To respond to questions, feedback and support requests.

2. Third-party services

PDF PowerKit uses or may use the following third-party services for specific app functions:

  • Google Mobile Ads – advertising services.
  • Google UMP – advertising privacy choices and consent management.
  • Google ML Kit – text recognition features.
  • Google Play services – document scanning functionality.
  • Google Play – app distribution and in-app updates.
  • YouTube – tutorial videos when you choose to view them.

These services may process information under their own applicable terms and privacy practices. Their actual data handling depends on the service and its configuration.

3. Your documents and privacy

PDF PowerKit is designed to process your selected documents locally. The app does not intentionally send your selected PDFs, images, Word documents or OCR text to a PDF PowerKit-operated document-storage server.

However, third-party services, Android system features, file-sharing applications and services you choose to use may handle information according to their own functionality and privacy practices.

4. Advertising privacy choices

You can review available advertising privacy choices through:

PDF PowerKit → Settings → Advertising Privacy → Manage choices.

Google's privacy-options form is available when required by the applicable consent configuration and Google's UMP service. Advertising choices and availability may depend on your location and applicable requirements.

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose or as required by applicable law. Local files, generated history and Vault information may remain on your device until deleted through the app or device controls, subject to applicable backups and copies.

We use appropriate measures intended to protect information. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
